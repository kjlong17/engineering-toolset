Before you use these tools

These tools are an engineering reference. For the inputs you enter, they compute design values from the Transportation & Street Technical Standards (TSTS, 2025 edition) and the TDOT, AASHTO, MUTCD and FHWA criteria they reference.

  • This is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, produced by or endorsed by the City of Franklin, Tennessee, or any agency that adopted the Standards.
  • It does not replace engineering judgment, a sealed design, or the approving agency's review.
  • The adopted Standards, and determinations by the approving agency, control over any result shown here.
  • Results are provided without warranty. Check every value against the cited source.
